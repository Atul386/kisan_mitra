Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This is a placeholder policy pending formal legal review before the app is published on the Play Store / App Store. It describes current data practices honestly rather than using a generic template. It will be replaced with a reviewed version before public launch.

What we store

Your farm, crop, task, expense, irrigation, fertilizer, spray, and market price records are saved on your device. Nothing is sent to a server yet — KisanMitra does not currently have a connected backend for this data, so it stays local to your phone.

Account & sign-in

You can use the app fully as a guest, with no phone number required. If you choose to sign in with a phone number, we use Firebase Authentication to verify it via a one-time code (OTP). We store your phone number only to identify your account for sign-in; we do not use it for marketing.

What we don't collect

No Aadhaar, PAN, or bank details. No access to your contacts. Location is only used if you choose to add it to a farm, so the app can show local weather — it is never shared with anyone else.

Photos

Crop check-in photos and expense receipt photos you choose to add are saved only on your device's local storage. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Notifications

Task reminders are scheduled locally on your device using your phone's own notification system. We do not use a push-notification service to send them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all data saved locally on your device. If you signed in with a phone number, contact us using the details below to request deletion of that account record.

Changes to this policy

As features that require a server (such as cloud sync or an AI assistant) are added, this page will be updated to describe exactly what is collected and why, and reviewed before the app is published publicly.
